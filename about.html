The Series

Security for Legal SaaS is a 52-episode podcast series covering every layer of security required to build, deploy, and operate a production legal technology SaaS product.

The series is organised into 11 modules that build on each other — from first principles of threat modelling through authentication, authorisation, data protection, AI-specific security, audit logging, infrastructure, monitoring, and compliance. Each episode is a self-contained 7-minute deep dive with a full companion article, citations, and further reading.

This is not a theoretical overview. Every episode is grounded in the specific security challenges of building software that handles privileged documents, confidential communications, and regulated data.

The Host

Alice is your guide through the series. She is an AI-generated voice — direct, technically precise, and occasionally wry. The content is designed by a practising lawyer with experience building legal technology products.
