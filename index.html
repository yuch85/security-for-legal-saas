Security for Legal SaaS

A podcast series covering every layer of security required to build, deploy, and operate a production legal technology SaaS product. From first principles of threat modelling through to SOC 2 audits and incident response runbooks — each episode is a self-contained deep dive, typically 7–9 minutes, that builds on the last. Module 1 — Foundations: Thinking Like an Attacker

Episode 1

What Is a Threat Model?

Why “we’ll add security later” is the most expensive sentence in software. The four questions every threat model answers. Assets, adversaries, and attack vectors for a product that handles privileged documents. A real-world example: an adversary-supplied contract containing hidden instructions.

Episode 2

The STRIDE Framework

Six categories of threat — Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege — each demonstrated through legal tech scenarios. Walking through a STRIDE analysis of a document review workflow.

9:14

Episode 3

Attack Surfaces in Legal Tech

Comprehensive attack surface mapping for legal AI SaaS. Document ingestion as critical attack vector. The distinctive threat profile where users handle confidential information belonging to adversarial parties.

Defence in Depth

The medieval castle model applied to SaaS architecture. Practical layering from network perimeter to audit logging. Assume-breach mentality and the single-provider trap. Case study: plaintext API key turns SQL injection into account takeover.

Reframing confidentiality, integrity, and availability for legal technology. Privilege-preserving architecture that goes beyond encryption. Case studies: Panama Papers, DLA Piper NotPetya, Grubman Shire Meiselas & Sacks.
